# Demo help desk triage
Synthetic training data for fictional Cedar Works.

Priority 1 means a company-wide critical service outage. The on-call technician
acknowledges it within 15 minutes and provides updates every 30 minutes.
Priority 2 means a department-wide degradation with a workaround; acknowledgement
is within one business hour. Individual password assistance is Priority 3 unless
there is evidence of a broader incident. Never ask a user to provide their password.
Ticket searches in this demonstration do not contain any real tickets or identities.
